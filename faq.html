Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our Spanish lessons and methodology.

How do the online classes work?

Our classes are 100% live via Zoom or Google Meet. You will practice conversational skills in real-time with an expert native tutor tailored entirely to your schedule and goals.

What materials do I need for the lessons?

Just a reliable internet connection, a computer or tablet with a webcam, and a microphone. All learning materials, worksheets, and digital resources are provided by us for free.

Can I reschedule a class if I get busy?

Yes, absolutely! We know life happens. You can reschedule any class with up to 24 hours' notice through our system at no extra cost.

Is the trial lesson completely free?

Yes, 100% free with no commitment required. It's a 25-minute introductory session to evaluate your current level, understand your goals, and let you experience our interactive method.
